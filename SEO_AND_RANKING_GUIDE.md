# Google Search Ranking & Backlink Strategy for ShalaSahayak.in (शाला सहायक)

This comprehensive guide outlines step-by-step instructions for getting **https://shalasahayak.in/** indexed on Google Search, achieving top rankings for Rajasthan teacher queries, and generating high-quality backlinks.

## 1. Domain Setup & Google Search Console Verification

### Step 1: Point Your Domain DNS to Cloud Run / Hosting
Ensure your domain registrar (GoDaddy, Namecheap, Hostinger, etc.) has the proper `A` and `AAAA` or `CNAME` records pointing to your application IP.

### Step 2: Register on Google Search Console
1. Go to [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console).
2. Add Property: Choose **Domain** (`shalasahayak.in`) or **URL Prefix** (`https://shalasahayak.in`).
3. Verify ownership via DNS TXT record or HTML file upload.
4. **Submit Sitemap**: In the left sidebar, click **Sitemap**, enter `sitemap.xml` (full URL: `https://shalasahayak.in/sitemap.xml`), and click **Submit**.
