2. Information We Collect (जानकारी जो हम एकत्र करते हैं)

Shala Sahayak is designed to operate primarily offline and client-side to ensure maximum security for your official data.

  • Local Storage State: All calculations, teacher records, school profiles, seating arrangements, and forms you fill in are saved directly in your browser's local storage (Local Browser Storage). We do not transmit or store this sensitive data on our servers.
  • Log Files: Shala Sahayak follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.
