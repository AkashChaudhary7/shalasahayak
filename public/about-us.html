About Us (हमारे बारे में)

Empowering Rajasthan Educators with Seamless Technology

Shala Sahayak (शाला सहायक) is a comprehensive, open-source web application designed exclusively to support school administrators, PEEO officers, teachers, and school staff across Rajasthan.

Our Mission (हमारा उद्देश्य)

शिक्षण और विद्यालयी कार्यों को सुगम, त्वरित और त्रुटिहीन बनाना ही हमारा ध्येय है। Rajasthan school administration involves multiple complex calculations—such as the 7th Pay Matrix fixation, ACP files, daily Mid-Day Meal logs (MDM), Board exam seating plans, and official relieving duties. Shala Sahayak condenses hours of clerical efforts into instant 10-second operations.

Key Features We Offer (प्रमुख विशेषताएं)

  • PEEO Orders Generator: Generate beautiful official duties, relieving and workload orders in a single click.
  • MDM Calculator: Automatically computes powdered milk, sugar distribution, wheat/rice amounts, and cooking costs with interactive logs.
  • ACP & Pay Fixation: A robust calculator matching Rajasthan's 7th Pay Commission rules.
  • Board Exam seating arrangements: Effortlessly design seatings, generate door slips, and output print-ready formats.
  • 100% Privacy & Offline-First: All records are saved locally in the browser. No login required and no server-side leakage of official documents.

Who We Serve

This toolkit is utilized daily by thousands of teachers and school heads across Jaipur, Jodhpur, Ajmer, Udaipur, and all corners of Rajasthan to stay fully aligned with the official Shivira Calendar and Shala Darpan data.
